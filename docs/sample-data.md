# Sample data provenance

The brief forbids real customer data in portfolio demos, and is right to. This file
records where every sample document came from, so the demo stays defensible.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**No real customer data is present in any of these.** The LIC wordings are product
documents, not policy records: the Schedule pages are blank templates with placeholder
labels (`Policy Number:`, `Name, Gender and Address of the Proposer`) and no personal
details, premium amounts or policy numbers filled in.

## Why the LIC documents are not committed

`samples/*.pdf` is gitignored. These are third-party publications and this repository is
public, so redistributing them is not ours to decide — the engineering artefacts
(`evals/questions-lic.yaml`, `docs/experiments.md`, `evals/results/`) carry everything a
reader needs to understand or audit the benchmark without the PDFs themselves.

Consequence worth stating: **the headline benchmark cannot be reproduced from a clean
clone** without obtaining the two wordings. That is why the synthetic suite exists as a
deterministic fixture — it runs anywhere, and `tests/test_chunking_real.py` reproduces the
LIC documents' structural traps via `build_lic_shaped_policy` so the chunking regressions
are covered in CI without the originals.

## Rules

1. Public policy wordings only. Insurers publish IRDAI-filed wordings as public PDFs.
2. Record the product, UIN and source in the table above before ingesting.
3. **Check redistribution terms before committing any PDF.** Prefer a download script or
   a generated fixture over vendored binaries.
4. No customer names, policy numbers or PII — including in screenshots and the demo video.
5. If a document turns out to contain filled-in personal data, it does not go in
   `samples/` at all, gitignored or not.
